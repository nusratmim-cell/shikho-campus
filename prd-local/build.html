UI & engineering build specs

Cross-cutting contracts for UI and engineering. Module pages own product behaviour. Wireframes own layout. This page owns breakpoints, notifications, print/PDF, permissions, session, and deletion.

Hard layout rules

  • At < 1100px the module sidebar becomes a drawer — same components, not a second product.
  • Minimum tap target: 44×44 CSS px for any primary action on phone and tablet.
  • Nothing important is hover-only. Focus-visible outlines are required on every interactive control.
  • Orientation change must not lose form drafts, quiz answers, playback position, or grade edits.

Toasts & in-product notifications

Events that change a person’s next action need a visible confirmation. Module briefs name the event; this section specifies the UI pattern.

PatternWhenBehaviour
ToastPayment confirmed · mark posted · hold cleared · sheet certified · export readyTop-right (desktop) / top-full (phone). Auto-dismiss after 5 s. Pause on hover/focus. Stack max 3; oldest drops first. One primary link optional (“View receipt”).
Inline bannerBlocking hold · eligibility refusal · provider down · finance staleStays until the condition clears or the user acts. Names the office and the clearing action. Never a toast alone for a blocker.
Rail / inboxNotices issued by offices · advising replies · system announcementsPersistent list. Unread count is informational — never a sidebar badge (badges are blockers only).

Channel priority (engineering)

When an event must reach the person: in-app toast/banner first → push (if allowed) → SMS (university-configured critical only) → email (receipts, digests). Deduplicate by event id so the same payment confirmation is not pushed three times.

Example toast copy “Payment of ৳28,500 confirmed. Your finance hold is clearing.” · “CSE 215 midterm marks are posted.” · “Grade sheet for CSE 215 · Sec A certified.”

Shared error & refusal states

Payment and hold flows already carry strong refusal copy in the module briefs. These patterns apply everywhere else. A disabled button with no explanation is a defect.

SituationWhat the person seesMust include
AI Tutor cannot answer from material“Your course materials do not cover this. Ask your teacher on the course discussion, or try a different question.”Deferral · next step · no invented content
AI refuses graded work“I will not write your assignment. I can explain the concept from your slides.”Refusal · useful alternative
File upload fails“Upload failed — connection lost after 2.1 MB. Try again. Your draft is still here.”What failed · what is kept · retry
Offline attendance save“Saved on this device. Will sync when you are online.”Local keep · sync promise
CSV import rejects rows“412 of 430 rows loaded. 18 rejected — download the list.”Counts · per-row reason · download
Recording / video unavailable“Recording is not ready yet. You will be notified when it is.”Honest state · no broken player
Empty by term phase“No classes scheduled — registration for Summer 2026 opens on 2 June.”Term context · dated next step
Permission denied“You do not have permission to certify grades. Ask your chairperson.”Named capability · who can help

Some artefacts must leave the browser as a printable page or downloadable PDF. Layout is part of the product, not an afterthought.

Print CSS rules

  • Hide navigation, demo strips, toasts, and AI rail on @media print.
  • Black text on white; no background fills that waste ink except the university crest and certification stamp.
  • Page breaks never split a student row mid-table; repeat table headers on each printed page.
  • Admit card and receipt must remain readable when downloaded on a phone and opened offline.

Multi-role model

One person may hold more than one role in the same university (common: TA who is also enrolled; advisor who also teaches; RA under a supervisor). Appointments expire; the profile keeps history — STU-PRF-01.

RuleBehaviour
One accountOne identity, one sign-in. Roles are dated appointments on that person, not separate logins.
Active roleMasthead shows a role switcher when the person has 2+ roles. Switching changes sidebar, home cockpit, and AI entry — not the identity.
Default roleLast used role for this device, else the role with the most urgent blockers.
Conflict of interestA person must not grade, take attendance, or access submissions for a section where they are enrolled as a student. The system refuses those actions with a named reason.
TA vs facultyTA never appears as teacher of record on the certified sheet. Powers come only from the appointment; posting remains faculty-only.
Advisor vs chairAdvisor may approve a plan inside policy; prerequisite override, overload and eligibility appeal escalate via STU-ADV-04 chair serial — advisor cannot override alone.
RARA grants no course workspace. Timesheets need supervisor approval; closed appointments remain on the profile.
AI modeFollows the active role: student → Tutor, faculty/advisor → Assistant (scoped), admin → Analyst. No AI for TA, RA or coordinator.
AuditEvery privileged action records both person id and active role at the time.
Role switcher copy “Acting as: Student” · “Switch to Faculty” · On conflict: “You cannot mark this section while you are enrolled in it.”

Session duration & sign-in

Universities use their own identity provider. Shikho Campus holds a session after successful SSO return.

SettingDefaultNotes
Idle timeout30 minutes without interactionConfigurable per university (15–120 min). Warn at 2 minutes remaining.
Absolute session lifetime12 hours from sign-inEven with activity, re-auth after 12 h. Configurable 8–24 h.
Exam / quiz lockSession must stay valid for the attempt windowIf session expires mid-quiz: answers already saved are kept; student re-auths and resumes with remaining time.
Remember this deviceOff by defaultIf university allows: refresh token up to 14 days; still requires IdP policy.
Concurrent sessionsAllowedSign-out-all available on profile. Payment and certify actions may re-prompt.
IdP unreachableBlock new sign-inExisting valid sessions continue until idle/absolute timeout. During exams: show “University sign-in is unavailable — contact invigilator” rather than a silent failure.
Token typeOpaque server session + short-lived API tokenImplementation detail for engineering; not JWT-as-sole-authority for privileged writes.
Idle warning copy “Your session will end in 2 minutes. Stay signed in, or save your work.”

PDPA deletion cascade

Bangladesh PDPA obligations and university record-retention duties conflict in places. The product must support lawful erasure requests without silently destroying academic integrity records the university is required to keep.

Who may trigger what

ActionWho initiatesWho executes in productResult
Access / portability requestStudent (or authorised agent)Coordinator packages; registrar may approve releaseExport of own profile, enrolments, receipts, certified results — not other students
Correction requestStudentCoordinator / registrar workflowOfficial name, DOB, photo: request only — never self-edit of sealed fields
Erasure requestStudent via university processRegistrar / data-protection officer path — not self-serve deleteSee cascade below
Tenant offboarding wipeUniversity contract endShikho platform admin under contractSeparate runbook; outside day-to-day RBAC

Soft delete vs anonymisation vs retain

Data classOn erasure requestWhy
Marketing / optional contact prefs, Tutor chat history, Momentum, device push tokensHard delete within 30 daysNot required for academic record
Profile photo, alternate email, emergency contact (non-official)Hard delete or clearNot needed once identity is sealed/anonymised
Official identity fields tied to transcript (name, student number, programme)Retain for university retention period (minimum 5 years typical) or anonymise only if law + university policy allow and transcript obligations are metIntegrity of certified results
Marks, attendance, certified sheets, payment receipts, audit log of who certifiedRetain (append-only). Link to anonymised subject id if identity is erasedCannot rewrite academic history
AI indexes / embeddings derived from personal chatDelete and reindex without that contentMust not keep erasable personal data in vector stores
BackupsExpire on backup rotation schedule; no restore into live of erased personal chat/prefsDocumented retention for backups

Cascade checklist (engineering)

  1. 1
    Registrar / DPOApproves erasure ticket with scope and legal basis.
  2. 2
    SystemFlags person erasure_pending; blocks new Tutor history and marketing use.
  3. 3
    SystemDeletes or clears erasable classes (chat, Momentum, tokens, optional contacts).
  4. 4
    SystemPurges personal content from AI indexes; job is idempotent and logged.
  5. 5
    SystemWhere policy requires, replaces display name with anonymised label on non-official surfaces; certified artefacts keep required legal identity per university policy.
  6. 6
    SystemWrites audit event: who approved, what classes deleted, what retained, timestamp.
  7. 7
    CoordinatorCan export the erasure certificate for the university file — cannot reverse without a new lawful process.
Never Self-serve “delete my account” that wipes certified marks · Coordinator hard-deleting a person without registrar approval · Analyst answers that re-identify an erased subject from “anonymised” fragments.
