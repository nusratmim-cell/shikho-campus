The Coordinator module

The department office and records desk for a private university term: load clean records, flag unstaffed sections, publish the class routine, mirror finance dues, verify bank slips, process documents and clearances, send certified results to the registrar, and hand graduates to alumni.

Introduction to the Coordinator module

No AI mode. The Coordinator is clerical by design — import, verify, export, flag. There is no Tutor, Assistant or Analyst for this role. Faculty assignment and results certification stay with the Administrator. See Shikho Campus AI.

What this module is

The Coordinator module is the clerical and records operations layer of Shikho Campus. It is where the department office keeps the term’s machine running: students and offerings are loaded correctly, rooms have a routine, finance dues are mirrored faithfully, slips are verified, documents move through a queue, clearances gather stamps, and certified results leave for the registrar on time.

It does not govern academic judgement and it does not own money. The chair assigns teachers and certifies marks. Finance sets amounts and receives payment. The platform never holds student money. The coordinator’s question is: are the records ready, and what must I hand to whom?

Who uses it

Primary users are department coordinators, programme officers and records assistants — people who sit at a counter or a shared office PC, switch between a parent with a bank slip and a registrar deadline, and need screens that speak in office language: validate, verify, flag, prepare, send. Accounts liaisons use invoice sync and slip verification. Graduation desks use clearance and the graduation list. Junior staff may see a subset of queues; senior officers see the full desk.

A day in the life

It is the week before finals. The CSE coordinator opens Coordinator → Records desk dashboard. Finance last synced twenty-five minutes ago; fourteen bank slips wait; two certified batches are ready for the registrar; three sections are still unstaffed and already flagged to the chair. She verifies slips under Coordinator → Verify payments, matching each photo to an open due — she cannot type a new tuition figure even when a parent insists. She runs Coordinator → Sync invoices after finance posts a waiver. She builds an export under Coordinator → Send certified results to registrar and sends only certified sections; drafts are not on the list. She prepares three transcript requests now that holds cleared. She does not assign a teacher to the unstaffed lab — she only confirms the flag is still visible on the chair’s dashboard. By late afternoon the desk counts are down, students with verified payments see holds release, and no mark or fee amount originated from her keyboard.

COO-HOME-01 · Records desk dashboard

P0

What it does. Gives the department office one screen of clerical work waiting — imports to finish, unstaffed sections to flag, invoices to sync, bank slips to verify, document requests, clearances, and certified results ready to send to the registrar — without offering teacher assignment or fee authorship.

Why it exists

Coordinators are the operational backbone of a Bangladeshi private-university term. Their day is interruptions: a parent with a slip, a chair asking which sections lack teachers, a registrar deadline for results. Without a desk dashboard, urgent export sits behind routine filing. This screen answers: what must I hand to whom today?
