What this product is

A university department is one connected system. The attendance a teacher takes decides whether a student may sit the final exam. The fee a student pays decides whether they may register next term. The marks a teacher submits become a transcript only after the chairperson certifies them. Today these connections are held together by spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, printed lists and memory.

Shikho Campus makes those connections part of the software. One event — a student joining a class, paying a bill, or a teacher submitting grades — updates every place that depends on it, for every person who needs to know.

What the university gets: run a complete term online — teach, take attendance, assess, collect fees, advise students, certify results, and hand certified results to the registrar — at a cost a private university in Bangladesh can sustain, without the maintenance burden of running a self-hosted system.

Rules we never break

These are not preferences. Every feature in every module obeys them, and a change that breaks one of them is a defect, not an improvement.

#RuleWhat it means in practice
1The chairperson certifies; the teacher marksAn administrator can accept a grade sheet or send it back with a reason. There is no screen, and no other route, that lets an administrator change a student's mark.
2AI drafts; a person decidesAI may suggest a mark or write feedback. Nothing reaches a student until the teacher has looked at it and approved it under their own name.
3We never hold university moneyPayment goes from the student, through a licensed provider, into the university's account. We show the bill, start the payment, and record the result.
4Fee amounts come from financeNo one inside the platform can create or edit a charge. We mirror what the finance office decided.
5Nothing important is silently overwrittenMarks, attendance, payments, holds and approvals keep their full history, with who did it, when, and why.
6Every number can be explainedIf the system says a student is barred from an exam or has a certain grade, it can show the records that produced that result.
7Differences between universities are settings, not new softwareGrading scales, attendance thresholds, probation rules and fee gating are configured per university.
8Teachers never invite studentsClass lists come from the university's records. No class codes, no manual invitations.
9Effort may be encouraged; students are never ranked publiclyNo leaderboards, no public comparison of marks.
10It must work on every device people actually usePhone, tablet, laptop, desktop and classroom smart TV — one web product, responsive layouts, not separate broken experiences

Devices and responsiveness

Shikho Campus is a web application. The same product runs in the browser on phone, tablet, laptop, desktop and classroom smart TV. Responsiveness is not optional — every role’s critical work must be usable on the device that person has in hand.

Hard requirements

  • One codebase and one design system scale across breakpoints — not a “mobile site” and a separate “desktop site” that drift apart.
  • Layouts reflow: sidebars become drawers on small screens; tables scroll horizontally or stack; video and live class never clip under chrome.
  • Touch and mouse both work. Nothing important requires hover-only.
  • Text remains readable on a 5-inch phone and on a 55-inch classroom display without a special “TV mode” that loses features.
  • Weak mobile data is assumed. Essential actions stay available when video quality must drop.
  • Orientation change (portrait ↔ landscape) does not lose form input, playback position, or draft marks.

Commercial model — three editions

Editions gate capability, not integrity — every edition obeys every product rule.

EditionWhat it isIncludes
EssentialsThe system of recordCourses, content, assessments, grades, directory, records operations
LiveEssentials + real-time teachingLive classes (Green Room → broadcast), recorded lectures with synced transcripts

Pricing is agreed in the sales conversation and is never used as pressure inside the product. The editions screen states the tier the university is on; it does not block work with upgrade prompts.

Product principles (binding)

Every screen obeys these constraints.

#PrincipleWhat it means
1Every number traces to a real inputIf a number cannot be traced, it is cut or honestly labelled — never fabricated
2Gamify effort, never gradesMomentum is private; no leaderboard; compete only with your own last week
3The AI drafts; the human decidesGrounded and/or human-approved; these controls stay on and cannot be switched off
4The administrator certifies; they never authorCertify or return — never edit a mark
5Oversight is aggregate, not surveillantA teacher’s private Momentum is never visible to the administrator
6Govern and operate are different jobsChair decides; coordinator operates; neither is cluttered with the other’s work
7One object, one designDirectory, Offering, Profile, term cockpit are shared components with role-appropriate actions
