Overview · University activity map

Everything that happens in a university

This page lists every real activity that takes place in a private university in Bangladesh — from the day a student applies to the day they collect a certificate as an alumnus — and says which module handles it, which feature covers it, and whether we build it now or later. If an activity is not on this list, it is missing from the product.

How to read this page

ColumnMeaning
ActivitySomething a real person actually does on campus.
Who does itThe person responsible in a normal Bangladeshi private university.
ModuleWhich of our four modules owns it.
FeatureThe feature that delivers it.
StageNow = first release · Next = second wave · Later = planned · Outside = stays in another system, we only read or show the result.
The honest boundary Some activities belong to systems the university already owns — the student information system, the accounts software, the bank. For those we do not rebuild the activity; we show it, act on it, and hand information back. Those rows are marked Outside and the connection is named.

F · Money

The rule that never changes Shikho Campus never holds university money. Fees are calculated by the university's finance system; payment travels from the student, through a licensed payment provider, straight into the university's bank account. We show the bill, start the payment, record the result, and clear the block.

What we build, and when

StageWhat it coversWhy this order
NowRecords loading, offering and staffing, teaching, content, attendance, assignments, quizzes, grading, grade submission, certification, exam eligibility, results to registrar, fees and online payment, holds, advising, notices, policy settingsThis is a complete working term. A university can run a full semester without spreadsheets.
NextExam schedule and duty, question paper support, documents and clearance, scholarships, discipline cases, teacher evaluation, thesis supervision, semester drop and readmission, AI helpThese are the office activities that surround the term.
LaterOutcome-based education reports, accreditation evidence, clubs and events, transport and medical, alumni, convocationValuable, but no university will refuse to buy without them on day one.
OutsideAdmission processing, payroll, procurement, HR, research management, library circulation, actual bankingThese already exist at every university. We connect to them instead of replacing them.
How to use this page in a sales conversation When a university asks "does it handle X?", find X in these tables. The answer is always one of four honest things: we do it now, we do it next, it is planned, or it stays in your existing system and we connect to it.
